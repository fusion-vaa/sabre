Table C - Authorized Airport List​

The following table lists all airports where Delta is currently authorized to conduct scheduled passenger operations and categorizes each airport into three categories, defined below, for each aircraft type in the fleet. Note that the table may exclude airports from historic timetables that Delta no longer serves, or airports that are included in historic timetables from other Delta-heritage operators, and it does not include columns for retired aircraft types or Delta Connection aircraft. When operating those aircraft, select a column for an aircraft that best matches the type you are flying. For example, and MD80 flight could consider A319/A320/A321/B737 columns, or an L1011 could consider A330/A350/B767 columns, etc.

The purpose of this table is to provide our members with a reference to aid them in selecting appropriate alternate airports when required (see [link to relevant section in Ch 3]).

Definitions:
  • Regular Airport (R): On Line. An airport used for scheduled operations.
  • Refueling Airport (F): On Line. An airport approved as an airport to which flights may be dispatched for a tech/fuel stop.
    • Note: It is not possible to dispatch a flight in vAMSYS with a planned tech/refueling stop, so this category is of limited utility in a flight simulation context.
  • Alternate Airport (A): Off Line. An airport at which an aircraft may land if a landing at the intended airport becomes inadvisable.

Note the use of "On Line" and "Off Line" in the definitions above. These categories provide an indiciation for which airports Delta currently has scheduled service (On Line) and which airports it does not (Off Line).

When using this table to select an alternate airport, pilots should consider the following factors:

  1. Is your aircraft approved to use the selected airport for your intended purpose?
  2. Is the selected airport On Line or Off Line? Significant preference should be given to On Line airports.
  3. Is the selected airport within a reasonable distance from your intended destination, or within a reasonable distance of your planned route for enroute alternates/diversions?
  4. Is the weather at the selected airport suitable? (See [link to relevant section in Ch 3])
